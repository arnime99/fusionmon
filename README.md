# Fusionmon

A Fabric mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1** that adds Pokémon fusions to **Cobblemon 1.8.1**, inspired by Pokémon Infinite Fusion.

## Features

- **Fusion Crystal**: right-click it to pick two Pokémon from your party and fuse them, or pick a fusion to split it back apart. Each fusion or split consumes one crystal.
- Fusion name, types and base stats are calculated from the head and the body, Infinite Fusion style. Level, IVs and EVs are averaged; you choose which nature and ability to keep.
- Fusions battle with their fused types and stats.
- Fusions evolve through either part, and learn level-up moves from both.
- Splitting gives both Pokémon all the experience earned as a fusion.

## Crafting

Fusion Crystal (crafting table): redstone in the bottom-left corner, lapis lazuli in the center and one of copper ingot, iron ingot, gold ingot, diamond or netherite ingot in the top-right corner. The accepted materials are the item tag `fusionmon:fusion_crystal_materials`, so datapacks can change them.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric Loader
- Fabric API
- Cobblemon 1.8.1

Install it on both the client and the server.
